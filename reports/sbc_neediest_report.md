## Methodology

### Composite Need Score

The composite need score weights four factors from ACS tract-level data:

1. **Poverty Rate** (×3) — Proportion of population below poverty line
2. **Unemployment Rate** (×2) — Proportion of labor force unemployed
3. **Low Income** — Inverted median household income (lower = higher score)
4. **Elderly Population** — Median age above 30 (higher = more elderly)
5. **Food Access** — Tract flagged as low-income + low food access
