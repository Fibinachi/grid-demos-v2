## Methodology

### Composite Opportunity Score (0-100)

| Factor | Weight | Data Source |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **Immigrant presence** | 40% | ZIP-level Hispanic% + Asian% (ACS 5-year) |
| **SBC decline** | 40% | County SBC adherent change 2010->2020 (ARDA) |
| **Population reach** | 20% | 15km radius catchment (Census 2020 tracts) |

Each factor normalized to 0-1 range then weighted. Immigrant population within 
approximately 15 minute drive estimated as: `catchment_pop_15km * ZIP_immigrant_pct`.
