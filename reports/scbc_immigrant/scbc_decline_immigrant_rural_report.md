## Methodology

### Composite Rural Opportunity Score (0-100)

| Factor | Weight | Data Source |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **Immigrant presence** | 35% | ZIP-level Hispanic% + Asian% (ACS 5-year), capped at 15% rural threshold |
| **SBC decline** | 30% | County SBC adherent change 2010->2020 (ARDA), capped at 40% |
| **Population reach** | 15% | 15km radius catchment (Census 2020 tracts), capped at 200K rural scale |
| **Facility viability** | 20% | Building sq ft, parking spots, capacity estimate from property data |

Rural-adjusted thresholds reflect the reality that immigrant percentages and 
total populations are lower outside metro areas, but facility costs and 
competition are also lower -- creating sustainable conditions for immigrant 
congregations with smaller numeric reach.

### Geographic Diversity Capping

Results are capped at **3 churches per county** to ensure geographic diversity 
across rural South Carolina. Without this cap, high-scoring counties like Newberry 
would dominate the list. The final 25 represent 11 counties, 
giving SCBC actionable options statewide.
